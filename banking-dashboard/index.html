Key takeaways

  • Digital leads: mobile app and online banking together account for over two-thirds of feedback — and score the highest satisfaction.
  • Regional gap: North America leads on NPS while Asia shows the fastest improvement trend over the last 12 months.
  • Opportunity: call-center feedback skews negative, suggesting the biggest service-design win is in assisted channels.
